Content integrity

Verify authenticity

Every article and every feed we publish is digitally signed. If even one character is changed after publication, verification fails. Checking happens entirely in your browser; nothing you paste or upload is sent anywhere.

How it is signed

  • ML-DSA-87 (NIST FIPS 204, security category 5) — a post-quantum signature that a future quantum computer cannot forge.
  • Ed448 (RFC 8032) — a classical signature alongside it. Both must be valid, so breaking one algorithm alone is not enough.
  • SHA-512 fingerprint of the exact published bytes, covered by both signatures together with the date and address.
