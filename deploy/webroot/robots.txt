# Headline Recorder robots.txt (copy to the web root; a static file wins over WordPress's virtual robots.txt).

# Policy (owner decision): search engines and AI search, answer and citation services may crawl and quote our
# journalism, so readers who ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot or Apple Intelligence about Pakistan's
# economy are pointed to Headline Recorder. Google-Extended, GPTBot, ClaudeBot and Applebot-Extended (the AI training
# tokens) are allowed as well, which keeps the newsroom eligible for grounding in Gemini and other assistants.
# Bulk dataset builders and unattributed scrapers are refused.
#
# Every crawler named in the first group follows the same rules as "*" (a crawler obeys only the most specific group
# that names it, so each one is listed here rather than in a group of its own without the exclusions).

User-agent: MistralAI-User
Disallow: /wp-admin/

Disallow: /readme.html
Disallow: /license.txt
# Internal search results and comment-reply duplicates. Other query strings (e.g. utm_ tags) stay crawlable: their
# canonical tag points at the clean URL, and CSS/JS with ?ver= must stay fetchable for rendering.
Disallow: /?s=
Disallow: /*?s=
Disallow: /*&s=
Disallow: /search/
Disallow: /*?replytocom=
# Signature checks use query strings (/verify/?sig=...); the page itself is public.
Disallow: /verify/?
Allow: /signed/
Allow: /.well-known/pqsig-keys.json
# Account pages (/account/...) and the reading dashboard are not blocked on purpose: they send "noindex", and a
# crawler has to fetch a page to see that. Blocking them here would let their bare URLs show up in results.

# Bulk dataset builders and scrapers that do not send readers back.
